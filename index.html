Mumen

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026
Developer: Leonardo Granados Sánchez

Mumen helps you organize subjects, assignments, and scores. This policy explains how the app handles your information and how to contact its developer.

Information stored on your device

Mumen stores the subjects, assignments, point values, scores, and other information you enter on your device. It also stores identifiers, creation dates, subject-creation history, deletion markers, and synchronization metadata needed to apply the app’s limits and synchronize reliably.

Mumen may retain local snapshots and recovery copies to protect your information during synchronization and conflict resolution. These copies may include earlier versions of subjects and assignments.

Optional iCloud synchronization

The free version stores your app data locally. Mumen Full supports synchronization through Apple’s CloudKit service using the private database associated with your iCloud account.

When synchronization is active, Mumen sends subject and assignment data and the associated history and synchronization metadata to your private iCloud database. The app uses an iCloud account identifier to prevent it from mixing data belonging to different accounts. The developer does not operate a separate server for your subjects and scores and does not have access to other users’ private CloudKit databases.

Apple processes iCloud information under its own terms and privacy policy. Your device and iCloud backups may also contain app data, depending on your Apple settings.

Purchases

Apple processes the one-time Mumen Full purchase. Mumen uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to verify Full access and restore purchases. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide the developer with sales and financial reports through App Store Connect.

Advertising, analytics, and sharing

Mumen does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs. The developer does not sell your personal information or use your subjects, assignments, or scores for advertising. Apple may separately process App Store, purchase, and diagnostic information according to your settings and its privacy policy.

Retention and your choices

You can edit and delete subjects and assignments within Mumen. With Full synchronization active, those changes are synchronized with iCloud. Deleting an item from the active list does not necessarily erase every copy immediately: deletion markers, creation history, synchronization checkpoints, local recovery snapshots, and device or iCloud backups may remain.

Deleting Mumen from a device removes its local app data; offloading the app does not. Deleting the app alone does not delete its iCloud data or existing backups. You can manage Mumen’s iCloud storage and device backups through Apple’s account and device settings, where available. Data retained in iCloud may download again when you reinstall the app and synchronize.

If you want to stop synchronization or need help removing data, contact the developer before changing your iCloud settings. The developer cannot directly erase information in your private iCloud database. Apple controls retention of its purchase and service records.

Support and privacy requests

If you email ljgssupport@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot issues, and retained only as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. Support email is handled through Gmail and is subject to Google’s privacy policy.

Please avoid sending sensitive information or unnecessary student names and scores. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address, subject to any applicable retention requirements.

Children’s privacy

Mumen does not require a separate developer-managed account and does not request a child’s name, age, or contact information. Subject and assignment information follows the local and private-iCloud storage practices described above. Parents or guardians may contact the developer with privacy questions.

GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes as described in its privacy statement. Clicking an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Mumen’s features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
