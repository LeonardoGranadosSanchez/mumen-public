Common questions

What does the free version include?

Free allows three lifetime subject creations and up to five assignments per subject. A subject can be renamed during the first 24 hours after its creation. Assignment edits and score updates remain available, and your data is stored locally.

Deleting a subject does not restore a lifetime subject slot. Deleting an assignment makes room for another assignment within that subject.

What does Mumen Full unlock?

Mumen Full is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited subject creation, assignments, and renaming, plus iCloud synchronization across your devices. It is not a subscription.

How do I restore Mumen Full?

Open Mumen, tap Full, then select Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase for App Store purchases.

If Full still does not appear, email support with the message shown in the app. Do not share your Apple Account password or payment details.

Why is iCloud synchronization paused?

Mumen pauses synchronization when it encounters a problem or detects an account change. Your local data is kept. Check your internet connection, confirm you are signed into the intended iCloud account, and tap Retry.

If it stays paused, email support with the exact message shown beneath the sync status. Please contact support before deleting the app or its iCloud data, especially if the device contains unsynchronized changes.

Why do my devices show different data?

For synchronization, both devices need Mumen Full and the same iCloud account. Open Mumen on each device while connected to the internet and check the synchronization status.

The Apple Account used for App Store purchases and the account used for iCloud can be different. Restoring Full verifies the purchase; it does not move data between iCloud accounts.

What should I do when changes need review?

Tap Review in the sync status area. Compare the saved versions and read each option before applying a resolution. If you are unsure which version contains the information you need, contact support before confirming.

Will I lose existing assignments if I no longer have Full?

Existing subjects and assignments are retained. Subjects above the free assignment limit can still be edited, scored, and reduced by deletion. Adding new assignments is restricted by the free limit. iCloud synchronization requires Full.

Does deleting the app delete my iCloud data?

Deleting the app removes its local app data, but does not by itself delete information stored in iCloud or existing backups. Data stored in iCloud may return after reinstalling and synchronizing. See the privacy policy’s retention and deletion section for more information.

How can I understand the score symbols?

Tap the information button (ⓘ) on Mumen’s main screen to open the score-symbol help.
